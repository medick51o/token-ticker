    02

    How a job moves.

    STEP 1
    Pick the project.Something to build or fix: a broken setup, a new tool or an app I want. I work out what done looks like.
    STEP 2
    Split up the legwork.Grok Bot hands the research and scripts to Cursor agents, Claude Code and Codex.
    STEP 3
    Get a second opinion.On the hard calls, models from other labs check the work. Nobody grades their own homework.
    STEP 4
    Own the fix.I check what comes back, make the call and close it out.
    “For the calls that matter, I don’t trust one model. I ask several, from different labs, without letting them see each other’s answers. Then I decide.”
